Welcome to QuickFavorites!

This folder IS your favorites menu. Everything in it appears when you click the "Desktop >>" button.

  * Add a shortcut, file or folder here and it shows up in the menu straight away.
  * Sub-folders become cascading menus.
  * Hover a folder to drill down, or double-click / right-click it to open it in Explorer.
  * Right-click the "Desktop >>" button to lock or reset its position, open this folder, or exit.

Everything in here is just sample content. Delete it and add your own favorites.
